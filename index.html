Why computational methods?

General trend across disciplines

The capacity to collect and analyze massive amounts of data has transformed such fields as biology and physics. But the emergence of a data-driven “computational social science” has been much slower.
--- Lazer (2009)

Drivers of the development

  1. Data availability: texts, networks, images, video
  2. Improved tools: network analysis, automated text analysis, vision-language models etc.
  3. Processing power: affordable (cloud) computing, sharing platforms

What is computational communication science?

Computational Communication Science

  1. Large and complex data sets
  2. Consisting of digital traces and other “naturally occurring” data*
  3. Requiring algorithmic solutions to analyze
  4. Allowing the study of human communication by applying and testing communication theory

Why Computational Communication Science?

  1. "Embarrassment of Riches": Explosion of data available, but only so much you can read (manually)
  2. Limitation of traditional methods: eg issues with self-reported data
  3. Advantages of digital traces: unobtrusive data collection, ecological validity, new perspectives on old phenomena

Challenges

  1. Data quality: bigger =/= better
  2. Privacy and ethics
  3. Often require high level of technical skills
